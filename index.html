This website is part of my neuroplasticity regime.

As a person who is disorderedly anxious, and this manifests with ocd and depression, I've tried a lot of things.

This is the newest one.

I'm reprogramming the ocd part of my brain by doing the same tasks every day - and thats the only rule. Not doing them well, successfully, right or anything else that triggers my whole hullabazoo of a brain to follow the usual neural pathways of anxiety, depression, helplessness. Just doing them.

These include but are not limited to:

Drawing, writing a short story, working out, getting educated in marketing and social engineering, writing down one job I could do for one month

And, of course, building this website.

 

It's very much under construction, but i made it and im making it better every day, nudging my brain into creation vs destruction mode.

I'm happy to say its working.
