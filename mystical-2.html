Agnostic Theism


I choose not to ponder things whose existence cannot be epistemologically established.

You can't prove the non existence of God. So i am agnostic. 

The mystical is a powerful tool in self reflection, community and more. I like to believe in something, so I am a theist. 

That makes me an agnostic theist.

My god is ITHUIGAN.

About ITHUIGAN


There's some personal lore attached to this word, but it can besummarized like this:

ITHUIGAN is the simultaneous existence of everything and nothing, a condition in which totality and absence are not opposites but coexist.

If we choose to take this definition as fact, something interesting can happen.

ITHUIGAN is simultaneously all that exist, and all that dont exist.

ITHUIGAN is simultaneously the proof that god exists, and the proof that god doesn't exist.

ITHUIGAN is a god, and ITHUIGAN isn't a god.

When ITHUIGAN, then:

The phrase "I don't believe in a god named ITHUIGAN" means, at the same timew, "I believe in a god named ITHUIGAN".

And that's pretty much why i believe in a god named ITHUIGAN. Because I actually don't, which means, by ITHUIGAN definition, that i do. 

Oops! I invented a noun, and, with it, a god! O_O

Why this is important to me:

 

I can not suspend my dispelief enough to believe in a higher being who cares about me. I just can't. It doesn't work for me. 

This creates difficult situations, though. Like, having to find a meaning in life on my own. Or, not being able to self soothe by praying.

ITHUIGAN is everything and nothing at the same time, so, it can be:
"Life has inherent meaning, and it doesnt have any inherent meaning"

With ITHUIGAN, life has meaning - if i choose to. 

With ITHUIGAN, I don't have to actually believe that some god exists to pray to them. When i pray to ITHUIGAN, I could be praying to any deity that wants to help me, or I could be talking to myself. In any case, I get in touch with my feelings, I express myself I feel better about things i can't control.

And that's what it's about.

So, yeah. With ITHUIGAN, I can help bring some whimsy, some of the divine, some meaning to my life, without having to subscribe to organized religion or leave my epistemological way of thinking behind. 

ITHUIGAN is brings magic to my life - and I fucking need it.
