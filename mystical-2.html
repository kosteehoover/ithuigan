mystical

I choose not to ponder things whose existence cannot be epistemologically established, so i am agnostic. 

I like to believe in something, so I am a theist. 

That makes me an agnostic theist.

The mystical is a powerful tool in self reflection, community and more.

Thich is why I have my own god, and their name is ITHUIGAN.

ITHUIGAN can be summarized in one sentence: 

"ITHUIGAN is simultaneously all that exist, and all that dont exist."

 

Why this is important to me:

I can not suspend my dispelief enough to believe in a higher being who cares about me. This creates difficult situations, like not being able to self soothe by praying.

But, by choosing to believe in ITHUIGAN, someone that is is simultaneously all that exist and all that don't exist, I pray to everything and nothing.

With ITHUIGAN, I don't have to actually believe they exist to pray to them. When i pray to ITHUIGAN, i could be praying to any deity that wants to help me, or i could be talking to myself. In any case, I feel better about thing i can't control- and that's what it's about.

With ITHUIGAN, life has meaning - if i choose to. 
ITHUIGAN is everything and nothing at the same time, so, it can be:
"Life has inherent meaning, and it doesnt have any inherent meaning"

So, yeah. With ITHUIGAN, I can help bring some whimsy, some of the divine, some meaning to my life, without having to subscribe to organized religion or leave my epistemological way of thinking behind. 

ITHUIGAN is brings magic to my life - and I fucking need it.
